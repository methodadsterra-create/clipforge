ClipForge

Free, open-source short videos. Runs on your phone, no account.

Your videos are stored only on this phone, never on a server. Anything you don't download within 24 hours is permanently deleted and can't be recovered.

AI helper (optional, free)

Get one free at aistudio.google.com/apikey. It is saved only on this phone. ClipForge reads the video's caption and writes fresh ideas. It does not download the video. A short clip that performed well, up to 18 MB. Only use videos you own or have permission to use.
When you use AI, your reference video and text are sent to Google's Gemini API with your own key. Everything else stays on this phone.
Make it with any free text-to-speech tool, then upload it. Without it, the video is silent and timed by reading speed.
Or choose your own photo or video clip. Keep this tab open and the screen on while the video is made.
