How to get your free key (2 minutes)

An API key is like a password that lets ClipForge ask Google's Gemini AI to write your script and speak it. Google gives every person a free daily allowance, so it costs you nothing.

  1. Open aistudio.google.com/apikey and sign in with your Google account.
  2. Tap Create API key. If it asks, choose Create API key in new project.
  3. Tap the copy icon next to your new key.
  4. Come back here, paste it in the box below, and you are ready. It is saved only on this phone.

Good to know

  • Never share your key or post it online. Anyone with it can use your allowance. If it leaks, delete it on the same page and make a new one.
  • It is free within Google's daily limits. Do not turn on billing unless you want to pay for more.
  • On Google's free tier, the text you send may be used to improve Google's products, so keep private details out of your videos.
  • If you see a "limit reached" message, wait until tomorrow or make another key.
